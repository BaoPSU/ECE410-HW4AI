Why the area shrank despite adding pipeline registers
=====================================================
The 3-stage pipeline lets yosys + ABC optimize each stage independently.
CF07's single combinational block forced a wide flat netlist with a deep
4-level argmin tree fanning into 16 kdist accumulators - one big AIG that
ABC couldn't break apart. Splitting at the kdist→argmin boundary plus the
mid-tree boundary halves the per-stage logic depth, so techmap can pick
smaller, faster gate variants for each stage. Plus DIST_W 20→18 shaves
two bits off every accumulator, mux, and comparator on the fan-out cone,
which compounds across the 16 parallel kdist computations.

Net: +693 sequential cells (pipeline regs) but -9,358 combinational cells
(better mapping of smaller stages) → 55 % total cell-count reduction.
