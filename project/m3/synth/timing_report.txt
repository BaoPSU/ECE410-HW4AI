Interpretation
==============
Timing closes at typical (TT 25°C 1.8V) and fast (FF -40°C 1.95V) corners with
**3.13 ns of positive slack** to spare on the worst path. The slow-slow corner
(SS 100°C 1.6V) misses by ~3 ns; the design would close all corners at a
~13 ns / 76 MHz clock target. For the M1 PIM chiplet I/O budget (16 TB/s
HBM3 via UCIe @ 100 MHz interface clock), nominal closure is the required
target and is met.

Compared to the CF07 unpipelined baseline (WNS = -31.53 ns, TNS = -662.68 ns,
22 setup-failing endpoints, ~24 MHz no-pipeline ceiling), the 3-stage
pipelined M3 design closes 10 ns at typical/fast with 3.13 ns headroom -
exactly what the cf07/synth/m3_plan.md projected.
